What an incredible 48 hours at the GDG AI Seekho Builders Day 2026 Hackathon at the National Incubation Center (NIC) Islamabad! 🚀

Our team took on the **Mohalla Mind** track, focused on Financial Inclusion. We asked ourselves: How can we make the invisible, informal gig economy visible in our local neighborhoods? 

Pakistan has over 12 million home-based workers—tailors, bakers, and tutors—who rely purely on word-of-mouth. Meanwhile, residents struggle to find trusted local help during rush seasons like Eid. 

To solve this, we built **Hunar Naqsha** 🗺️✨ — *Apni gali ka hunar, apni marzi ka daam.*

It’s a mobile-first neighborhood skill marketplace where residents post needs, and local workers bid with their own prices. But we didn't stop at just a directory. We integrated Google's **Gemini AI** to act as a "Mohalla Mind". Our AI fuses data on open needs, worker availability, and seasonal calendars to proactively predict skill shortages in specific zones, automatically drafting WhatsApp mobilization notices to the community before a crisis happens! 🤯

A huge thank you to the GDG team and NIC for hosting such a brilliant event. I'm incredibly proud of what we accomplished in such a short time.
