## Slide 1: Title Slide
**Headline:** Hunar Naqsha
**Sub-headline:** Apni gali ka hunar, apni marzi ka daam. (Your neighborhood’s skill, your own price).
**Visuals:** A mockup of your app's home screen on a mobile phone next to the logo.
**Speaker Notes:** "Salam everyone. Today we are presenting Hunar Naqsha, a hyperlocal skill marketplace built for the Mohalla Mind track. We are turning the invisible neighborhood economy into a visible, trusted network."

---

## Slide 2: The Hidden Problem
**Headline:** 12 Million Invisible Workers
**Bullet Points:**
- Pakistan has a massive informal gig economy (tailors, home bakers, electricians).
- Discovery is entirely word-of-mouth.
- Residents overpay or can't find help during rush seasons (Eid, Exams).
- Workers have no digital storefront and no public trust score.
**Visuals:** Two icons: A confused resident looking for a tailor, and a tailor sitting idle in the next street.
**Speaker Notes:** "If someone in G-10 needs a tailor before Eid, they ask on WhatsApp groups. Meanwhile, a skilled home tailor just one street over has no work because she has no shop. The supply is there, but discovery and trust are broken."

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
**Headline:** A Digital Marketplace for the Mohalla
**Bullet Points:**
- **Hyperlocal:** Residents post needs in their specific zone.
- **Fair Bidding:** Workers see nearby jobs and bid their own prices.
- **Built-in Trust:** Post-job reviews build a public reputation score.
**Visuals:** A 3-step graphic: Post Need ➔ Receive Bids ➔ Chat & Hire.
**Speaker Notes:** "Hunar Naqsha solves this. Residents post exactly what they need. Workers in the same zone bid on it with their own prices. And most importantly, every completed job builds a public 5-star trust profile for that worker."

---

## Slide 4: AI & The "Mohalla Mind"
**Headline:** Predicting Skill Shortages with Gemini AI
**Bullet Points:**
- **Fuses Signals:** Analyzes open needs, worker supply, and seasonal urgency (e.g., Eid in 10 days).
- **Intelligent Alerts:** Turns zones Green, Yellow, or Red based on gap severity.
- **Actionable Mobilization:** Drafts bilingual WhatsApp messages to recruit workers before a crisis hits.
**Visuals:** The Architecture diagram showing data flowing into the Gemini Agent, outputting a "Red Zone Alert".
**Speaker Notes:** "What makes this a 'Mohalla Mind' is our Gemini AI integration. It doesn't just list jobs; it reasons. If it sees a spike in tailoring jobs, notices zero bids, and knows Eid is approaching, it turns the zone Red and automatically drafts a WhatsApp message for residents to share, mobilizing the community to find more tailors."
